Our Services

1. Animal Rescue

We respond to calls about stray, injured, and abandoned animals in Tembisa. Our team provides safe transport to our shelter for care.

2. Veterinary Care & Rehabilitation

Every animal gets a health check, vaccinations, deworming, and treatment. We partner with local vets to nurse them back to health.

3. Adoption Services

We screen potential adopters and match them with the perfect pet. Adoption includes sterilization, microchip, and starter food pack.

View Animals for Adoption

4. Community Education

We run free workshops in Tembisa schools about responsible pet ownership, sterilization, and animal welfare.

5. Donations & Volunteering

We accept food, blankets, and monetary donations. Volunteers help with walking dogs, cleaning, and adoption days.
