About Us

Our Mission

Tembisa Animal Rescue was founded in 2018 to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned and abused animals in the Tembisa community. We believe every animal deserves a second chance at a loving home.

What We Do

  • Rescue: We rescue stray, abused, and surrendered dogs and cats
  • Rehabilitate: We provide medical care, food, and shelter
  • Rehome: We find forever homes through adoption drives

Our Team

We are a team of volunteers, vets, and animal lovers who work tirelessly to give these animals hope. Every donation and adoption helps us save more lives.
